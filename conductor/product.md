# Product Definition: AI Receptionist (n8n + Retell AI)

## Vision
A production-ready AI receptionist template that handles incoming calls, extracts information, and integrates with business workflows using n8n and Retell AI.

## Core Features
- **Inbound/Outbound Call Handling:** Automated voice interactions via Retell AI.
- **n8n Integration:** Real-time data processing and workflow automation.
- **Production-Ready Pillars:**
    - **Error Handling & Fallbacks:** Ensuring calls never "die" during failures.
    - **Lead Qualification:** Intelligent scoring of callers before booking.
    - **Real-Time Syncing:** Live availability checks via Cal.com/Calendars.
    - **Monitoring:** Detailed transcript logging and sentiment analysis.
- **Human Handoff:** Seamlessly transfer calls to human agents when needed.
- **Post-Call Automation:** Automated SMS follow-ups and CRM logging after every call.
- **Lead Capture:** Automatic extraction of caller details and intent.
- **Appointment Scheduling:** Integration with calendars (via n8n).

## Target Audience
- Small to medium businesses looking for automated customer service.
- n8n developers seeking a robust voice AI template.
